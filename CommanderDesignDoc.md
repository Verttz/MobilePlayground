## RACE 1 — The Sidewinder Syndicate
**Theme:** Your cowboy snakes — baseline race  
**Role:** Baseline / Familiar  
**Complexity:** Low–Medium  
**Fantasy:** Capitalism, lawlessness, guns, contracts  
**Plays like:** Humans in TANO

### Core Mechanics
- **Economy:** Credits
- **Build Style:** Buy buildings directly
- **Units:** Gunslingers, trains, turrets, bounty drones
- **Theme Hook:** Everything is a deal, contract, or bounty

### Strategic Identity
- Flexible, reactive
- Strong midgame
- Commander abilities are very "active screen impact"

### Commanders

#### 1. Marshal Coil
- **Ability:** Call down orbital "Dead Noon" revolver barrage
- **Twist:** Defensive bonuses near buildings
- **Playstyle:** Hold-the-line, turret-heavy

##### Passive
- **Bulwark of the Line:** Units and turrets near friendly buildings gain bonus armor and light regeneration. Effect scales with the number of nearby buildings.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Coil Watchtower:** Defensive turret that grants a small defense aura to nearby units.
- **Fortified Depot:** Supplies ammo and armor plating; nearby units gain armor; unlocks turret upgrade options.
- **Contract Militia Post:** Periodically hires militia based on available credits; militia expire after their contract.
- **Bounty Telegraph Office:** Marks enemies; nearby turrets prioritize marked targets and grant small credit payouts on kill.

#### 2. Hexshot Harlan
- **Ability:** Ricocheting cursed bullets
- **Twist:** Killing enemies refunds partial commander skill cooldown
- **Playstyle:** Aggressive snowball economy

##### Passive
- **Hex Refund:** Enemy kills refund a small percentage of commander ability cooldown and grant minor bonus credits.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Cursed Gunsmith:** Enables enhanced ricochet mods; increases projectile bounce count for nearby units.
- **Gambler's Den:** Chance-based economy: on kill, chance to grant extra credits or a short damage buff.
- **Ricochet Range:** Field training structure that causes friendly projectiles to bounce off enemies/terrain an extra time.
- **Bounty Bank:** Stores bounty payouts; streaks increase interest and periodic credit dividends.

#### 3. Train Baron Slith
- **Ability:** Summon armored supply trains
- **Twist:** Buildings connect via "rails" for bonuses
- **Playstyle:** Infrastructure-focused, slow but unstoppable

##### Passive
- **Rail Efficiency:** Rail-connected buildings gain +throughput and +range; supply trains deliver buffs faster to connected nodes.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Rail Hub:** Connects nearby buildings into a rail network granting shared bonuses.
- **Armored Trainyard:** Periodically dispatches supply trains that grant shields and resources along rail routes.
- **Freight Depot:** Converts credits into timed resource shipments; boosts economy cadence when rail-connected.
- **Signal Tower:** Increases range and damage of rail-connected turrets; enables path control via signals.

#### 4. Deadman's Hand
- **Ability:** Choose a building/unit to buff for a short duration; if it survives, it gets a permanent buff. If not, you permanently lose a portion of income
- **Twist:** You cannot sell or refund buildings. Credit income scales upward over time
- **Playstyle:** High stakes commitment and bluffing

##### Passive
- **All-In Economy:** Credit income scales upward over time; cannot sell/refund. Failed commitments reduce income, but successful gambles add stacking economic bonuses.

##### Unique Buildings
- **High-Stakes Saloon:** Activate to buff one target building; survival grants a permanent buff, failure causes permanent income loss.
- **Bond Office:** Issue bonds for immediate income boosts at the risk of future penalties if commitments fail.
- **Insurance Bureau:** Protects a single building from destruction once per wave; charges a recurring premium.
- **Grave Ledger:** Records failed gambles; mitigates future income loss slightly and unlocks recovery options.

#### 5. Doc Venom
- **Ability:** Heal or overclock units with injections
- **Twist:** Units can overheat and explode if abused
- **Playstyle:** High-risk micro

##### Passive
- **Controlled Dose:** Units receive small periodic healing; overclock threshold is reduced for faster bursts but increases overheating risk.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Venom Clinic:** Emits a healing aura; removes minor debuffs from nearby units.
- **Overclock Lab:** Temporarily boosts attack speed/damage; builds heat over time requiring management.
- **Med Drone Bay:** Spawns repair drones to service buildings and units during combat.
- **Cooling Tower:** Dissipates heat from overclocked units, reducing explosion risk.

#### 6. The Sheriff (AI)
- **Ability:** Autonomous justice drone
- **Twist:** Less player input, more automation
- **Playstyle:** "AFK commander"

##### Passive
- **Rule of Law:** Automatically tags high-threat enemies; justice drones deal bonus damage to tagged targets with minimal player input.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Justice Drone Foundry:** Produces autonomous justice drones that patrol and engage enemies.
- **Law Office:** Converts enemy infractions into fines (credits) and applies debuffs to marked enemies.
- **Surveillance Post:** Auto-detects and reveals hidden enemies; improves drone accuracy.
- **Holding Cell:** Temporarily detains marked enemies, delaying their advance.

#### 7. Bounty Queen Rattle
- **Ability:** Mark priority targets
- **Twist:** Killing marked enemies gives unique buffs
- **Playstyle:** Target-priority gameplay

##### Passive
- **Queen's Mark:** Killing marked targets grants stacking teamwide buffs (damage and move speed) that decay slowly over time.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Bounty Board:** Select and mark priority targets; grants bounty bonuses on completion.
- **Hunter's Lodge:** Units trained nearby gain bounty perks, dealing extra damage to marked enemies.
- **Tagging Station:** Applies marks in an area; extends mark duration and improves tag potency.
- **Trophy Vault:** Stores trophies from marked kills; unlocks rotating unique buffs.

#### 8. Goldfang
- **Ability:** Converts corpses into credit piles
- **Twist:** Encourages letting enemies reach choke points
- **Playstyle:** Greedy, risky economy abuse

##### Passive
- **Greedy Gain:** Corpses converted near choke points yield bonus credits and small temporary unit buffs; higher rewards come with greater risk.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Corpse Collector:** Converts nearby corpses into credit piles over time.
- **Choke Point Cartel:** Buffs defenses and credit yields for structures placed at choke points.
- **Smelter Mint:** Processes collected materials into credits with increasing returns the longer it operates.
- **Risk Exchange:** Grants higher payouts when enemies reach deeper into defenses; slightly increases wave intensity.

## RACE 2 — The Helio-Swarm
**Theme:** Sentient solar insects  
**Role:** Momentum / Timing  
**Complexity:** Medium  
**Fantasy:** Light, growth, inevitability  
**Plays like:** A resource that expires

### Core Mechanics
- **Economy:** Solar Charge (decays over time)
- **Build Style:** Grow structures using stored light
- **Units:** Living mirrors, flare larvae, prism titans

### Strategic Identity
- You're racing the sun
- Power spikes followed by collapse
- Rewards tempo mastery

### Commanders

#### 1. The Dawnbinder
- **Ability:** Extend the current daylight phase, slowing Solar Charge decay for a short time
- **Twist:** Solar Charge decays much more slowly, but max storage is capped lower than other commanders
- **Playstyle:** Steady, methodical, beginner-friendly. You're rewarded for efficient spending rather than explosive bursts. Strong early and midgame, weaker at scaling into absurd late-game builds

##### Passive
- **Sunsteady Flow:** During daylight, Solar Charge decay is reduced further and unit regen slightly increases near light structures.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Daylight Obelisk:** Strengthens daylight phases in a radius; reduces local decay and buffs unit attack speed.
- **Chrono Sundial:** Smooths income over time; extends daylight marginally when multiple are placed.
- **Photosphere Reservoir:** Stores excess Solar Charge with low leak; releases steady trickle to nearby buildings.
- **Radiant Outpost:** Increases vision and damage for light-aligned units; enhances daylight auras.

#### 2. Nova Herald
- **Ability:** Trigger a Solar Nova, instantly filling Solar Charge to max for a brief window
- **Twist:** Outside of Nova windows, Solar Charge generation is nearly zero
- **Playstyle:** All-in burst planning. You save builds, upgrades, and unit spawns for Nova moments, then scramble to survive the drought afterward. Extremely swingy and high-pressure

##### Passive
- **Nova Preparation:** Non-Nova periods accumulate “charge focus” stacks that amplify the next Nova’s damage and build-speed bonuses.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Nova Spire:** Increases Nova duration slightly and adds an overcharge damage pulse at start.
- **Burst Crucible:** Units spawned during Nova gain temporary double haste and lifesteal.
- **Timing Relay:** Synchronizes building activations to fire at Nova start; improves timing precision.
- **Overcharge Array:** Converts a portion of Nova overflow into shields for nearby units and structures.

#### 3. The Eclipse
- **Ability:** Blanket the battlefield in artificial darkness, empowering shadow-adapted units
- **Twist:** You generate power during darkness, not light. Daylight actively weakens your economy
- **Playstyle:** Inverted Helio-Swarm. You want shorter days, longer nights, and aggressive wave skipping. Forces players to fight the clock instead of riding it

##### Passive
- **Umbral Harvest:** During darkness, units and buildings siphon ambient power, granting small Solar Charge trickle and shadow damage.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Eclipse Generator:** Extends local darkness and boosts shadow unit stats.
- **Shadow Nursery:** Trains night-adapted units with bonus evasion during darkness.
- **Nightfall Ward:** Reduces incoming damage in darkness; increases Solar Charge generation from Umbral Harvest.
- **Obscura Prism:** Converts stray light into shadow buffs; improves darkness transitions.

#### 4. Prism Mother
- **Ability:** Shatter friendly units into refracted offspring when activated
- **Twist:** Most units split into weaker copies on death, increasing board coverage
- **Playstyle:** Board control and attrition. You trade unit quality for quantity, overwhelming enemies with space denial and gradual damage rather than raw power

##### Passive
- **Refraction Instinct:** Units have a small innate chance to split into two weaker copies on death; chance increases near prism structures.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Prism Nursery:** Increases offspring quality and spawn speed after shatter events.
- **Refraction Tower:** Emits splitting fields that boost the chance of refraction and add minor on-split damage.
- **Shard Hive:** Generates disposable shardlings over time; swarms excel at space denial.
- **Lightscatter Loom:** On unit split, grants brief movement and attack speed bursts to nearby offspring.

#### 5. Flare Architect
- **Ability:** Detonate a selected building, releasing stored Solar Charge as damage and buffs
- **Twist:** Replacing or removing buildings causes controlled explosions instead of refunds
- **Playstyle:** Planned obsolescence. You constantly rebuild your base, chaining destruction into offense. Strong players pre-plan layouts knowing they will blow them up later

##### Passive
- **Blueprint of Ashes:** Recently detonated sites grant a temporary zone buff (damage and haste) for new structures placed there.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Detonation Node:** Stores charge specifically for explosive releases; increases detonation radius and status effects.
- **Flare Foundry:** Builds structures faster; gains stacking output when placed on former detonation sites.
- **Rebuild Crane:** Reduces rebuild time globally and grants brief fortification to freshly placed buildings.
- **Thermal Converter:** Converts ambient heat from explosions into temporary shield and damage boosts for nearby units.

#### 6. Lux Harvester
- **Theme:** Hoarding light until it becomes dangerous
- **Mental Model:** Delayed gratification with instability
- **Ability:** Solar Compression — Condense all current Solar Charge into a single unstable core. Release it later to generate a massive burst of energy and damage
- **Twist:** Light Pressure — Stored Solar Charge no longer decays. However, holding too much charge causes periodic solar flares that randomly damage your own units and buildings, intensifying the longer you hoard
- **Playstyle:** Greedy tension management. You stockpile sunlight far beyond safe limits, deciding when the risk is worth it. Every second you wait increases both the payoff and the danger. Mental loop: "Can I survive one more flare to make this burst worth it?" This commander keeps Helio's time pressure, introduces risk through excess (not decay), and has no overlap with Nova Herald or Radiant Tyrant

##### Passive
- **Pressure Valve:** Reduces self-damage from solar flares slightly and increases burst output when releasing compressed light.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Compression Vault:** Safely stores massive Solar Charge; increases flare interval but boosts eventual release.
- **Flare Dampener:** Lowers flare damage in an area; improves survivability of economy cores.
- **Capacitor Garden:** Array of small capacitors that smooth release profiles into sustained buffs.
- **Light Siphon:** Slowly drains excess charge into nearby buildings’ performance without triggering flares.

#### 7. Radiant Tyrant
- **Ability:** Burn your own health to instantly generate massive Solar Charge
- **Twist:** Health is a primary resource. You regenerate slowly but are always near death
- **Playstyle:** Hyper-aggressive risk management. You're constantly trading survivability for momentum, living on the edge and daring the game to kill you

##### Passive
- **Burning Dominion:** The lower your health, the higher your damage and charge conversion efficiency; capped to prevent instant demise.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Hemolight Crucible:** Converts commander health into Solar Charge more efficiently when guarded; risky but potent.
- **Searing Shrine:** Grants damage buffs to allies at the cost of periodic health drain; synergizes with lifesteal effects.
- **Pain Engine:** Increases output when allies take damage; rewards constant combat.
- **Vital Furnace:** Converts incoming healing into temporary damage shields and Solar Charge trickles.

#### 8. The Last Sunset
- **Ability:** Trigger a final dusk, massively empowering all units as Solar Charge hits zero
- **Twist:** The lower your Solar Charge, the stronger your army becomes
- **Playstyle:** Reverse-scaling desperation. You intentionally run yourself dry, surfing collapse for power. Feels weak early, terrifying when mastered

##### Passive
- **Dusk Resolve:** Grants scaling damage and defense buffs as Solar Charge approaches zero; minor sustain added at critical levels.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Eventide Bastion:** Defensive structure that grows stronger as charge falls; ideal for last-stand zones.
- **Twilight Forge:** Enhances unit upgrades that scale inversely with charge; unlocks last-stand traits.
- **Last Light Banner:** Rally point granting morale buffs that intensify with low charge.
- **Collapse Engine:** Consumes remaining charge to grant a brief overwhelming push, then enters long cooldown.

## RACE 3 — The Void Union
**Theme:** Collective of black-hole laborers  
**Role:** Space manipulation  
**Complexity:** High  
**Fantasy:** Bureaucracy meets cosmic horror  
**Plays like:** Repositioning the battlefield

### Core Mechanics
- **Economy:** Mass
- **Build Style:** Compress space to place buildings anywhere
- **Units:** Singularity drones, gravity cannons

### Strategic Identity
- Control where enemies exist
- Non-traditional "lanes"

### Commanders

#### 1. Foreman Collapse
- **Ability:** Relocate a building instantly during combat
- **Twist:** Buildings have no fixed position and gain buffs when moved mid-wave
- **Playstyle:** Constant repositioning. You react to enemy paths in real time, turning the battlefield into a shifting puzzle instead of a static defense

##### Passive
- **Momentum Workflow:** Moving a building mid-wave grants a brief stacking productivity buff (damage/range) to that building.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Mobile Platform:** Specialized foundation allowing low-friction repositioning with minimal downtime.
- **Transit Gantry:** Reduces movement cooldowns and grants a short fortify after relocation.
- **Adaptive Rig:** Auto-adjusts stats (range/arc) based on latest position relative to enemy pathing.
- **Site Office:** Issues move orders more efficiently; global reduction to reposition penalties.

#### 2. Union Rep Null
- **Ability:** File a grievance that temporarily boosts all structures after a delay
- **Twist:** Most bonuses are delayed but permanent
- **Playstyle:** Long-term planner. You suffer early weakness for inevitable late dominance. Patience and scheduling matter more than reflexes

##### Passive
- **Paper Trail:** Each filed grievance adds a permanent minor buff to core stats across all structures.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Filing Station:** Speeds up grievance processing; increases permanence rate.
- **Arbitration Chamber:** Converts temporary boosts into permanent workplace standards.
- **Compliance Bureau:** Expands buff coverage radius and ensures consistent uptime.
- **Benefit Office:** Grants periodic universal small bonuses when enough grievances are logged.

#### 3. The Auditor
- **Ability:** Mark enemies for inspection, increasing Mass gained when they survive longer
- **Twist:** Enemies generate more resources the longer they exist
- **Playstyle:** Controlled greed. You intentionally avoid killing enemies too fast, balancing danger against economy growth. Extremely tense risk-reward gameplay

##### Passive
- **Extended Review:** Marked enemies grant bonus Mass per second alive and small intel pings on their pathing.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Assessment Tower:** Applies inspection marks and tracks survival timers.
- **Risk Desk:** Increases Mass yield from long-lived enemies but slightly buffs their HP.
- **Retention Field:** Slows marked enemies subtly to prolong audits.
- **Ledger Node:** Stores audit results; periodic payouts scale with cumulative inspected time.

#### 4. Gravemaster
- **Ability:** Deploy permanent gravity wells that bend enemy movement
- **Twist:** Enemies follow curved and looping paths instead of straight lanes
- **Playstyle:** Spatial domination. You design kill-zones that enemies can't escape, focusing on geometry over raw damage

##### Passive
- **Gravitic Memory:** Gravity wells become more potent each time enemies traverse them, increasing curvature and slow.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Gravity Well Anchor:** Core field generator defining path curvature; upgrades improve loop strength.
- **Orbital Lure:** Draws enemies into preferred arcs; synergy with anchors to form loops.
- **Vector Bastion:** Defensive node optimized for curved paths; increases kill-zone efficacy.
- **Path Shaper:** Fine-tunes local vectors; reduces pathing anomalies and improves well stability.

#### 5. Shiftwalker
- **Ability:** Instantly swap positions of enemy groups or structures
- **Twist:** Enemy formation integrity matters — clumped enemies are dangerous to themselves
- **Playstyle:** Disruption-heavy micro. You constantly scramble enemy plans, turning their strengths into weaknesses via forced repositioning

##### Passive
- **Swap Pressure:** Recently swapped enemies suffer disorientation, taking extra collision and environmental damage briefly.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Phase Relay:** Reduces swap cooldowns and extends swap range.
- **Crossroad Obelisk:** Creates intersection zones that amplify disorientation and collision effects.
- **Echo Node:** Records last positions to enable smarter multi-swap patterns.
- **Inversion Gate:** Temporarily flips ally/enemy speed modifiers in the swap area.

#### 6. Overtime Protocol
- **Ability:** Overclock all buildings, scaling their power with uptime
- **Twist:** Buildings grow stronger the longer they remain untouched, but reset if moved or replaced
- **Playstyle:** Defensive anchoring. You protect and nurture a few key structures instead of expanding wide, playing a fortress-style Void Union

##### Passive
- **Seniority Bonus:** Buildings gain stacking bonuses each wave they remain untouched; bonuses are preserved across minor repairs.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Tenure Core:** Centralizes uptime stacks, increasing growth rate for connected structures.
- **Maintenance Hub:** Prevents stack loss on minor faults; reduces downtime.
- **Productivity Beacon:** Amplifies output scaling based on cumulative uptime in the area.
- **Stability Lattice:** Grants damage reduction proportional to uptime stacks; discourages relocation.

#### 7. Zoning Commissioner
- **Theme:** Bureaucracy weaponized into map control
- **Mental Model:** Area denial and compliance enforcement
- **Ability:** Redraw Boundaries — Designate restricted zones on the battlefield. Enemies entering these zones suffer severe movement penalties and debuffs
- **Twist:** Compliance Cost — Restricted zones consume Mass continuously. Zones can overlap, compounding effects but increasing upkeep exponentially
- **Playstyle:** Territorial control and resource taxation. You don't move buildings or enemies — you make parts of the battlefield illegal. Victory comes from deciding which spaces are worth enforcing and which you let go. Mental loop: "Is this area valuable enough to keep regulated?" This commander reinforces Void Union's space manipulation identity and avoids tempo freezes or self-harm

##### Passive
- **Compliance Layering:** Enemies affected by multiple zones suffer compounded debuffs and generate audit intel drops.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Boundary Marker:** Establishes restricted zones; efficient upkeep.
- **Zoning Office:** Reduces Mass upkeep and enables layered compliance effects.
- **Permit Kiosk:** Temporarily suspends enemy abilities within zones.
- **Taxation Node:** Converts zone enforcement into periodic Mass gains when enemies remain inside.

#### 8. Singularity Prime
- **Ability:** Relocate and reshape a single massive megastructure
- **Twist:** You can only build one structure, but it absorbs others to evolve
- **Playstyle:** Boss-unit management. All strategy revolves around positioning and upgrading one ever-growing monstrosity

##### Passive
- **Event Horizon Growth:** Absorbing structures grants permanent bespoke traits; relocation adds temporary power spikes.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Assimilation Spire:** Enables absorption of nearby structures; defines trait categories.
- **Core Foundry:** Crafts permanent modules to attach to the megastructure.
- **Grav Control Nexus:** Improves relocation precision and grants brief invulnerability during reposition.
- **Stellar Feeder:** Channels ambient Mass into steady evolution; boosts trait scaling.

## RACE 4 — The Chorus of Many
**Theme:** Cloned consciousness hive  
**Role:** Sacrifice / Multiplication  
**Complexity:** Medium–High  
**Fantasy:** Identity erosion  
**Plays like:** Trading bodies for power

### Core Mechanics
- **Economy:** Voices (generated by dying units)
- **Build Style:** Structures are grown from sacrificed units
- **Units:** Echoes, Resonators, Choir Engines

### Strategic Identity
- Death is progress
- Loss is fuel

### Commanders

#### 1. The First Voice
- **Ability:** Empower all newly spawned units for a short time
- **Twist:** Early-game bonuses are massive, but do not scale
- **Playstyle:** Early dominance. You push hard to secure momentum, knowing you'll fall behind if the run drags on

##### Passive
- **Opening Chorus:** Newly spawned units gain brief stacking morale and speed buffs in the first minutes of a run.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Rally Ampitheater:** Boosts morale and spawn speed early; falls off later.
- **Fresh Echo Nursery:** Enhances starter unit stats for a limited time window.
- **Momentum Drum:** Grants global minor haste when many units are freshly spawned.
- **Primer Pavilion:** Early-game upgrade discounts to accelerate tempo.

#### 2. Echo Queen
- **Ability:** Command fallen units to repeat their last action
- **Twist:** Dead units still influence the battlefield
- **Playstyle:** Afterimage control. You plan unit deaths deliberately to create echoes that clog paths and deal delayed damage

##### Passive
- **Lingering Orders:** Fallen units leave short-lived command echoes that repeat attacks or blocks once.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Echo Lattice:** Captures last actions, replaying them as phantoms.
- **Afterimage Gate:** Increases duration and potency of echoes on death.
- **Resonance Archive:** Stores powerful echoes for manual release later.
- **Memory Pathways:** Imprints path-slowing echoes that persist, shaping enemy routes.

#### 3. The Conductor
- **Ability:** Trigger rhythm-based buffs by activating on-beat
- **Twist:** Perfect timing grants exponential bonuses
- **Playstyle:** Execution-heavy. Mechanical skill matters. Feels almost like a rhythm game layered onto strategy

##### Passive
- **Pulse Meter:** Grants small periodic buffs when actions align with global rhythm; perfect beats stack briefly.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Metronome Tower:** Establishes a global beat; increases benefit from perfect timing.
- **Sync Node:** Aligns nearby building activations to beat windows.
- **Crescendo Hall:** Amplifies buffs when consecutive perfect beats are achieved.
- **Staccato Forge:** Short, sharp boosts to attack speed on beat ticks.

#### 4. Silencer
- **Ability:** Erase enemy drops in exchange for raw stat buffs
- **Twist:** You gain no external resources, only internal power
- **Playstyle:** Minimalist brutality. Fewer choices, higher stakes. You're powerful but inflexible, relying on raw force

##### Passive
- **Mute Yield:** Converting drops to power grants stacking flat damage and armor; eliminates pickups.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Null Vault:** Absorbs all drops in radius; increases local stat floors.
- **Quiet Foundry:** Converts ambient resource nodes into permanent stat lines.
- **Silence Field:** Prevents enemy drops; grants temporary attack buffs when drops would have occurred.
- **Brutalist Keep:** High base stats; rejects all refunds and rebates.

#### 5. Splitmind
- **Ability:** Duplicate your commander ability with reduced strength
- **Twist:** You can stack weaker versions of your own effects
- **Playstyle:** Combo-centric. You layer overlapping buffs and effects, building momentum through synergy instead of singular power spikes

##### Passive
- **Fractal Thought:** Duplicated effects gain incremental efficiency per stack; caps prevent runaway scaling.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Mirror Loom:** Generates weak copies of recent commander effects.
- **Overlay Hub:** Increases cap for simultaneous weaker stacks.
- **Feedback Array:** Each stack slightly increases duration/effect of the next.
- **Echo Scribe:** Records sequences of effects to replay later at reduced strength.

#### 6. Dirge
- **Ability:** Release stored sorrow after mass unit deaths
- **Twist:** Power scales with how many allies died recently
- **Playstyle:** Catastrophe farming. You deliberately allow disasters, then retaliate with overwhelming bursts

##### Passive
- **Mourners' Weight:** Recent ally deaths grant temporary damage and resistance stacks; decay slowly.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Wailing Spire:** Stores sorrow; increases retaliatory burst potency.
- **Memorial Cloister:** Grants defense buffs to units spawned after losses.
- **Elegy Drum:** Converts recent death count into timed attack speed surges.
- **Black Procession:** Summons spectral reinforcements after mass losses.

#### 7. Archivist
- **Ability:** Record elite sacrifices to unlock permanent upgrades
- **Twist:** Certain losses are irreversible but rewarding
- **Playstyle:** Long-view optimization. You sacrifice short-term power to sculpt a stronger endgame

##### Passive
- **Ledger of Worth:** Elite sacrifices add permanent cataloged bonuses; future runs may inherit minor traits (if supported).

##### Unique Buildings
- **Archive Vault:** Stores records of sacrifices; unlocks upgrade tiers.
- **Curator's Hall:** Improves quality of recorded upgrades; favors rare units.
- **Testimony Chamber:** Converts recorded losses into universal small buffs.
- **Heritage Monument:** Enables carryover traits across sessions (meta-progression context).

#### 8. The Final Note
- **Ability:** Unleash a single reality-ending crescendo (1 time board wipe)
- **Twist:** After using it, you lose all commander abilities
- **Playstyle:** One-shot strategist. The entire run builds toward one perfect moment. Miss it, and you limp to the finish

##### Passive
- **Quiet Before:** Gradually accumulates calm stacks improving defense and focus until the crescendo is unleashed.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Crescendo Stage:** Amplifies final wipe potency; increases preparation speed.
- **Rehearsal Hall:** Grants minor sustained buffs while building toward the final note.
- **Silence Curtain:** Reduces incoming damage in preparation zones.
- **Final Baton:** After the wipe, grants limited fallback buffs to survive the aftermath.

## RACE 5 — The Scrap Ascendants
**Theme:** Self-upgrading machine cult  
**Role:** Customization / Evolution  
**Complexity:** Very High  
**Fantasy:** Industrial mutation  
**Plays like:** A build-your-own faction

### Core Mechanics
- **Economy:** Scrap
- **Build Style:** Modular buildings
- **Units:** Chassis with attachable parts

### Strategic Identity
- Every run becomes unique
- High planning overhead

### Commanders

#### 1. Assembler Alpha
- **Ability:** Add extra module slots to buildings and units
- **Twist:** Complexity replaces raw power — more options, harder choices
- **Playstyle:** Sandbox tinkerer. You constantly optimize layouts, rewarding deep system mastery

##### Passive
- **Modular Synergy:** Each unique module type equipped grants small cross-bonuses; diversity increases efficiency.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Module Forge:** Crafts advanced modules; reduces slot install time.
- **Integration Bay:** Improves compatibility between mismatched parts; lowers malfunction chance.
- **Optimization Lab:** Analyzes layouts; grants global micro-efficiency buffs.
- **Blueprint Library:** Unlocks rare module archetypes for deep customization.

#### 2. Rust Prophet
- **Ability:** Spread corrosive decay across enemies and structures
- **Twist:** Damage increases over time but never spikes instantly
- **Playstyle:** Inevitable rot. You play patiently, letting corrosion win wars you don't fight directly

##### Passive
- **Creep of Rust:** Corrosion applied by allies slowly intensifies and spreads to nearby enemies.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Oxidizer Tower:** Emits rust fields that increase corrosion stacks.
- **Corrosion Vat:** Converts corroded scrap into small periodic resource gains.
- **Pitting Array:** Causes armor pitting; reduces enemy resist over time.
- **Decay Relay:** Extends rust duration and propagation range.

#### 3. Patchwork King
- **Ability:** Fuse nearby buildings into hybrid constructs
- **Twist:** Fused structures cannot be undone
- **Playstyle:** Commitment-heavy experimentation. You gamble on powerful but irreversible combinations

##### Passive
- **Quilted Might:** Hybrids gain extra stats when made from diverse building types.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Fusion Loom:** Facilitates safe fusion; adds minor bonus traits to hybrids.
- **Patch Port:** Allows partial trait transfer between structures pre-fusion.
- **Graft Bench:** Enhances compatibility; reduces negative hybrid outcomes.
- **Crown Socket:** Grants a capstone bonus to fully fused constructs.

#### 4. The Reclaimer
- **Ability:** Dismantle structures for partial refunds
- **Twist:** Mistakes are recoverable, but refunds shrink over time
- **Playstyle:** Adaptive learning. Encourages experimentation early, then punishes indecision later

##### Passive
- **Early Return:** Higher refund rates in the early waves; decays as the run progresses.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Scrap Foundry:** Processes dismantled parts into higher-quality scrap.

#### 5. Overclocker
- **Ability:** Push units beyond safe limits
- **Twist:** Overclocked units suffer permanent degradation
- **Playstyle:** Short-term power addiction. You burn through units for momentum, managing collapse rather than preventing it

##### Passive
- **Heat Dividend:** Overclocking yields brief power surges followed by degradation; surges grant small teamwide haste.

##### Unique Buildings
- **Thermal Jack:** Raises overclock ceiling; increases heat management needs.
- **Stressor Rig:** Greatly boosts output for short windows; accelerates degradation.
- **Coolant Plant:** Slows degradation; reduces heat buildup.
- **Maintenance Den:** Restores minor durability post-overclock; can't fully reverse wear.

#### 6. Scrapchild

- **Twist:** Strength comes from unit density, not tech

- **Merge Yard:** Speeds merge times and improves result stability.
- **Collective Beacon:** Encourages clustering; increases synergy radius.
- **Trait Nursery:** Adds minor bonus traits when merging similar unit types.
- **Swarm Pen:** Generates fodder units to fuel merges.

- **Playstyle:** Meta-progression commander. Rewards long-term planning across multiple runs

##### Passive

- **Registry Core:** Records locked upgrades; improves inheritance strength.
- **Archive Backplane:** Enables synergy between locked upgrades.
- **Seed Vault:** Stores rare parts for future runs.
- **Protocol Relay:** Accelerates lock-in process and reduces risk.

#### 8. The Machine God
